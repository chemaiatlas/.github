# ChemAI Atlas

**Discover AI tools, models, agents, MCP servers, and open-source projects for chemistry.**

ChemAI Atlas is a curated directory for exploring artificial intelligence across chemistry, cheminformatics, molecular modeling, drug discovery, materials science, and related scientific fields.

🌐 **Website:** [chemaiatlas.com](https://chemaiatlas.com)

---

## Explore Chemistry AI

ChemAI Atlas helps researchers, students, developers, and industry professionals discover useful AI resources for chemistry.

We organize resources across several major categories:

- 🤖 **AI Models** — Molecular models, foundation models, property prediction, generation, retrosynthesis, and more
- 🧪 **AI Tools** — AI-powered applications for chemistry and scientific research
- 🤝 **AI Agents** — Autonomous and semi-autonomous agents for research workflows
- 🔌 **MCP Servers** — Model Context Protocol servers for chemistry and scientific applications
- 💻 **Open Source** — Libraries, frameworks, research code, and developer tools
- 📚 **Guides** — Practical guides, comparisons, and workflows for chemistry AI

---

## Why ChemAI Atlas

Chemistry AI resources are scattered across GitHub, research papers, product websites, model hubs, and developer communities.

ChemAI Atlas brings them together in one structured place.

Our goal is simple:

> Make chemistry AI easier to discover, understand, and use.
